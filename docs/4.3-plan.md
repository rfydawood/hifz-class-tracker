BreakLog 4.3: three parts in one release. Website first, then the Android app to the Hifz testers group.
Parts 1 and 2 are fixes; Part 3 is the new Appearance settings.

PART 1 - Teacher mode: close the loopholes
While a PIN is set and teacher mode is off, these must ask for the PIN (use the existing asTeacher() helper, as the ☰ menu does):
1. The Reports tab: add 'reports' to LOCKED_TABS, including Export CSV. When the tablet locks while Reports is open, go back to the Log tab (as Roster/Settings/Profile do now).
2. Day summary and Attendance today, from anywhere (the "Class ended" banner's View day summary, the ☰ menu, anywhere else).
3. resetDay(), including the banner's Reset day button. It deletes the day, so it matters most.
4. startPause() / startDueBreak() and endPause(), from the snack/lunch banners and anywhere else.
5. Attendance taps in tap(): before class, toggling present / not here; after class starts, marking a "Not here" student tardy; bringing back a student who "left for the day".
6. undo() for anything a student couldn't do themselves while locked. Undoing a break the student just started can stay open.
Students keep exactly two things: start a break, and Return. Go through every button and tap target in index.html that reads student data or writes data, and list in your summary what you locked and anything you deliberately left open, with the reason.
Auto-lock: add a Settings option "Lock again automatically" with Off / 2 / 5 / 10 minutes (default 5). The tablet relocks after that many minutes without a teacher action, with a short toast "Locked again". No change when no PIN is set.

PART 2 - "Back at" can't be in the future
Seen on the tablet: a student out for water since 2:09 was returned with Back at 2:30 while it was still 2:09, so the break was saved as ~21 minutes and flagged.
1. In saveBreakEdits() and the "Fix" editor for a logged break (editLoggedBreak and its save), refuse a start or end time later than now: "Back at can't be later than now." Keep the existing "before the time they left" check.
2. Show it live in recalcElapsed() as well ("Back at is later than now", in red).
