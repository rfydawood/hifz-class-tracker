## Part 1 - Teacher mode: the loopholes closed

With a PIN set and teacher mode off, each of these now asks for the PIN, does nothing if the PIN pad is closed, and goes ahead once the right PIN is typed. The check sits inside each function, so every button that reaches it (banner, ☰ menu, tab, or anything added later) is covered.

**Newly locked in 4.3**
- **Reports tab** (`'reports'` added to `LOCKED_TABS`) and **Export CSV** (`exportCSV`). Locking while on Reports goes back to Log, like Roster/Settings/Profile.
- **Day summary** (`openDaySummary`) - from the "Class ended" banner's *View day summary*, the ☰ menu, anywhere.
- **Attendance today** (`openAttendanceReview`) and changing a mark in it (`setStatus`).
- **Reset day** (`resetDay`) - the banner's *Reset day* button and Settings' *Reset the day*.
- **Snack / lunch / any scheduled break**: `startPause`, `startDueBreak` (the "is scheduled now" banner), `startScheduledBreak` (menu), `endPause` (the "End snack/lunch" banner and menu).
- **Start / end / resume class** from any button (`doStart`, `endClass`, `resumeClass`) - before, only the header button and the menu were covered.
- **Attendance taps on the tiles** (`tap()`): before class, marking present / not here; after the start, marking a "Not here" student tardy; bringing back a student who "left for the day".
- **Undo** (`undo()`) of a Return (it would reopen the break) or an attendance change. Undo of a break the student just started stays open.
- **A student's list of today's breaks** (`openStudentBreaks`, the "See or fix today's breaks" button on the break sheet, and the day summary rows) and its Save / Delete (`saveLoggedBreak`, `deleteLoggedBreak`; `editLoggedBreak` already asked).
- **Saving the out-student editor's corrected times** (`saveBreakEdits`) - the editor itself was already teacher-only; this is belt and braces.
- **Hiding the tab bar** (the down-arrow). It writes this device's setting and hides the padlock.

**Already locked before 4.3 (unchanged):** break minutes stepper, Fix / Cancel break (`editBreak`, `cancelBreak`), "left for the day" (`markLeft`), ☰ menu (`openDrawer`), the header Start/End button (`mainAction`), Roster, Settings, Profile (and everything in them: classes, schedule, limits, Delete my account, backup).

**Students keep exactly two things:** start a break (tap their name -> reason -> Start) and Return (tile or "Back in").

**Deliberately left open, and why**
- *Terms and privacy screen* (Accept / Sign out) - the 4.2 rule says it is never behind the PIN; it only appears when the account must accept new terms.
- *"Forgot the PIN?"* on the PIN pad - it is the way back in (it asks for the class's Google account).
- *The handle that brings the tab bar back* - otherwise a locked tablet with the bar hidden could not reach the padlock.
- *Undo of a break the student just started* - as the brief says.
- *The break-dots line on a student's own "Where is X going?" sheet* ("3 today · 12 min · 1 flagged") and the trip counts on the reason buttons - they are part of starting a break (they explain the daily limit) and show the same information as the ⚑ flag already on that student's tile.
- *The Log screen itself* (names, timers, Out right now, header counts) - it is the students' screen.

**Auto-lock:** Settings -> Teacher mode -> **Lock again automatically**: Off / 2 / 5 / 10 min, **default 5**. The tablet relocks after that many minutes without a teacher action, with the toast "Locked again" (and goes back to Log from a locked tab). A "teacher action" is anything that passes the PIN check, unlocking, or a tap/typing in Reports, Roster, Settings, Profile or the ☰ menu - a student starting or ending a break does not keep it unlocked. Store key `teacher.autoLock`, this device only, in the hydrate list. With no PIN set nothing changes and it never fires (the setting says to set a PIN first).
*Choice made:* the "last teacher action" clock is kept in memory, so reopening the app starts it over (no extra writes on every tap).

## Part 3 - Settings -> Appearance

Built to the two approved mockups (both read in full; the mockups' own colour values are used for all 11 themes). Behind the teacher PIN with the rest of Settings, **saved on this tablet only**. The app keeps its current names (BreakLog / "…'s Hifz Class" class titles, as today).

- **Colour theme:** 11 cards (mini header + three tiles) - Classic (default, exactly today's colours), Lapis and brass, Sand and sky, Garden, Night study, Emerald, Ottoman, Plum, Desert, Midnight, Plain grey - plus **Custom colour**.
- **Custom colour:** the 12 swatches; header, buttons and tabs are built around the pick (the derivation reproduces the mockup's Indigo example within a shade). Amber, orange and red are shown crossed out with "Amber, orange and red are kept for Out and Past the limit" and can't be chosen.
- **Light / Dark / Automatic** (Automatic follows the tablet and switches live). Night study and Midnight are always dark (the setting says so). Dark with a light theme uses Night study's dark surfaces and keeps that theme's accent. `--ink` is split: `--head` (header), `--btn` (dark buttons) and `--ink` (text) are separate tokens; the stylesheet's and the charts' hard-coded colours now use tokens (about 115 places), except white text, the break colours and the grey "left early" chip, which are the same in every theme.
- **Background behind the tiles:** Plain / Soft tint / Pattern / Custom.
  - **Pattern:** a picker of the 15 designs as small previews. Each is an inline SVG in index.html (no downloads; works offline), drawn in one colour: faint ink (10%) on light themes, gold #D9A84E (20%) on dark ones, as in the mockup.
  - The two **calligraphy** designs: brush-like shapes only, no letters or words. Refined from the mockup's outlines: simplified and redrawn as smooth curves (the diamond nib-dots kept sharp), and 35% smaller.
  - **Custom:** "Choose a photo" (opens the tablet's picker), shrunk to 1280px wide JPEG on the tablet, a **Fade** slider (50-95%, default 75%), **Remove**. Kept on this device only (Store), never uploaded or written to Firestore.
- **Where it shows:** every page follows the theme (Log, Reports, Roster, Settings, Profile, ☰ menu, sign-in/setup screens). The background shows only in the margins and gaps around panels and cards; names, timers, numbers, charts and lists stay on solid cards. With a background, the Settings column becomes a card (it has no cards of its own). Pop-up sheets and the PIN pad stay plain.
- **Never changes:** in class = calm, out = amber, past the limit = red (dark themes use the mockup's dark amber/red), and the five break colours.
- **Readability:** accent fills and accent text are nudged automatically until they read at 4.5:1; a test checks names, timers, header, tabs, report cards and roster text in every theme light and dark at both tablet sizes. *Noted, not changed:* the red "past the limit" text on its pink tile is today's colour pair at 3.9:1 (the brief says those colours never change); it is bold and it passed as "large/bold" (3:1) in the check.
- **Storage:** Store keys `ui.appearance` (JSON: theme, custom colour, mode, background, pattern, fade) and `ui.bgPhoto`, next to `ui.tabsHidden`, both in the boot hydrate list (with the localStorage fallback on native). Applied by a small `<head>` script before the page is drawn, so the old colours never flash; on Android/iOS the choice is also mirrored to localStorage for that first paint, and boot re-applies it from Store.
- **Organizations later:** `ORG_APPEARANCE` in `appearanceNow()` is the slot for an organization default (under each tablet's own choice) - not built. Note added to `docs/organizations.md` -> Themes.
