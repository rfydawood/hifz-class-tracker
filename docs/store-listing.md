### Play full description — 2075/4000

```text
BreakLog is a class break and attendance tracker for hifz teachers. Put it on the class tablet: a student taps their name to go out for a break and taps again when they come back. You see at a glance who is out, why, and for how long.

Made for a hifz class
• Break types for washroom, water, wudhu and other, plus assigned (excused) time that never counts against a student.
• Set a time limit and a number of trips a day for each break type, and see straight away when someone goes over.
• Whole-class breaks such as snack, lunch and Jumu'ah come up on time from your schedule.
• Mark who is here, who came late and who left early.

Five simple tabs
• Log: today's class, the student tiles and who is out right now.
• Reports: charts and numbers for today, yesterday, this week, last week or last month, for the whole class or one student. Time out of class, breaks, trips over the limit and attendance, with the change since the period before. Export to a spreadsheet (CSV).
• Roster: your class list with each student's week so far.
• Settings: class times, a usual day, weekdays that differ, one-off dates, and break limits.
• Profile: your account, backups and your classes.

More than one class
Add every class you teach, each with its own students, schedule and limits, and switch between them in a tap. Archive a class you no longer teach and bring it back any time.

Teacher mode
Set a 4-number PIN on the class tablet. Students can still start and end their own breaks, but settings, the class list and changes to records need the PIN. Forgot it? Sign in again with Google to reset it.

Works offline, in step everywhere
Sign in with Google and your classes are the same on your tablet, phone and computer. No internet in class? Carry on as normal; everything is sent when the device is back online.

Your data stays yours
No ads, no analytics and no tracking. Students never sign in or have accounts. Download a backup of every class at any time, and delete your account and all its data from inside the app.

Free to use. Questions or ideas: rfy.dawood@gmail.com
```

### Before submitting to Apple - check this

Apple's guideline 4.8 ("Login Services") says an app that signs people in
with a third-party service such as Google must also offer a sign-in option
that limits data to name and email and lets the person hide their email -
in practice, **Sign in with Apple**. The app currently has Google sign-in
only, so App Review may reject it until Sign in with Apple is added. Plan
for that before the first iPhone submission.

## Reviewer account (to set up before submitting)

Make a new Google account just for reviewers (not the owner's own), sign in
to the app with it, and add a practice class with made-up names only (for
example "Practice class" with students "Student A" to "Student F"). Leave
teacher mode off. Put its email and password in the two `[REVIEWER ...]`
lines above when filling in the store forms - never commit the password.
Google may ask a new sign-in on an unknown device for a code; turn off
2-step verification on this account so reviewers aren't blocked.
