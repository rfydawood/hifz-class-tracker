Delete your BreakLog account

You can delete your BreakLog account and everything it holds here, or from inside the app: Profile → Delete my account.

What is deleted

  • Every class your account owns, with every day, break and student in it
  • Your "My classes" list
  • Your Google sign-in for BreakLog. Any other device signed in to your account is signed out.

It happens straight away and can't be undone. Nothing is kept afterwards. If you belong to an organization that other people use too, you leave it, and its classes stay with the organization.

Keep a copy first

To keep your records, download a backup before you delete: open BreakLog on the website, sign in, then Profile → Backup → Download a backup.
