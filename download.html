Installing on Windows

  1. Open the downloaded file.
  2. If a blue “Windows protected your PC” box appears, click More info, then Run anyway. This shows because the app is new, not because anything is wrong.
  3. It installs and opens by itself, and adds Hifz Class Tracker to your Start menu.

Always up to date

The desktop app, the website and the tablet all show the same classes, live. The desktop app picks up every new version on its own.

On a tablet or phone?

Open the app in your browser and choose Add to Home Screen, or ask for an invite to the Android app.
